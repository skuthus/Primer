PRIMER: INSTALL GUIDE
=====================

Primer gives KOReader a Kindle-style interface: a library home, a reading
toolbar, quick settings, an Aa panel, dictionary cards, a notebook and
settings. It's an add-on for KOReader, the free e-book reader, so you need
KOReader working on your device first. This guide walks through both.


WHICH GUIDE IS YOURS?
---------------------
  Kindle, firmware 5.16.3 or newer ............ Guide A
    (Paperwhite 2021 and later, Kindle 2022 and later, Scribe, Colorsoft)
  Kindle, firmware older than 5.16.3 .......... Guide B
    (Voyage, Oasis, older Paperwhites and basic Kindles)
  reMarkable Paper Pro or Paper Pro Move ...... Guide C
  Kobo, PocketBook or another device .......... Guide D

To find a Kindle's firmware: open Settings > Device Options > Device Info.
The number after "Kindle" is the firmware version, for example 5.19.6.

Tested on: Kindle Voyage (5.13.6), Kindle Paperwhite 12th gen (5.19.6),
reMarkable Paper Pro Move. Primer needs KOReader 2026.07 or newer.


WHAT'S IN THE ZIP
-----------------
Download Primer-<version>.zip from the Releases page:
https://github.com/skuthus/Primer/releases

Unzip it on your computer. Inside are:

  koreader/      Primer itself, its fonts, sleep screens and a dictionary.
                 Merges into KOReader's own "koreader" folder.
  extensions/    Kindle only: the start-at-boot helper.
  documents/     Kindle 5.16.3+ only: the "Primer" item for the home screen.
  Primer - Install.txt   This guide.


=============================================================================
GUIDE A: KINDLE ON FIRMWARE 5.16.3 OR NEWER
=============================================================================

Step 1. Jailbreak the Kindle
  Primer can't do this part, and the right method depends on your exact
  model and firmware. Use the jailbreak wizard at
      https://kindlemodding.org/jailbreak-wizard.html
  and follow the guide it gives you, to the end.

  Important:
  - Do NOT update the Kindle's firmware before or during this. Amazon's
    updates close the holes jailbreaks use, and Kindles can't go back.
  - Keep Airplane Mode on until the guide tells you to turn Wi-Fi on.
  - The guide may ask you to fill the Kindle's storage first. Do it. You'll
    delete the filler files at the end.

  You'll know it worked when the guide's last step says so (on most methods
  the screen shows a "RESTARTING GUI" message and the Kindle comes back).

Step 2. Install KOReader
  On the Kindle, with Wi-Fi on:
  1. Tap the search bar on the home screen, type   ;kpm update
     and press Enter. Wait until you're back on the home screen.
  2. Tap the search bar again, type   ;kpm install koreader
     and press Enter.
  3. After a minute, a "KOReader" item appears in your library. Open it once
     to check KOReader starts, then exit it (menu > Exit).

Step 3. Copy Primer onto the Kindle
  1. Connect the Kindle to your computer with a USB cable. It shows up as a
     device (not a drive) with an "Internal Storage" folder.
       Windows: in File Explorer, under "This PC".
       macOS:   needs an MTP app, such as OpenMTP (free).
       Linux:   in your file manager (GNOME Files, Dolphin...).
  2. Open "Internal Storage". You'll see folders such as "documents" and
     "koreader".
  3. From the unzipped Primer folder, copy "koreader", "extensions" and
     "documents" into Internal Storage. They merge with the folders that are
     already there. If asked, replace existing files.

Step 4. Start Primer
  1. Unplug the Kindle.
  2. A "Primer" item appears in your library (give it a minute). Tap it.
     The old "KOReader" item opens the same thing; you can delete it.
  3. Primer sets itself up and opens your library. A welcome screen offers
     to start Primer whenever the Kindle turns on. Your choice; you can
     change it later in Settings > Device options.

Go on to "Adding books" below.


=============================================================================
GUIDE B: KINDLE ON FIRMWARE OLDER THAN 5.16.3
=============================================================================

Step 1. Jailbreak the Kindle, and install the hotfix and KUAL
  Use the jailbreak wizard at
      https://kindlemodding.org/jailbreak-wizard.html
  and follow its guide, including the "hotfix" (which keeps the jailbreak
  through restarts) and KUAL (the Kindle Unified Application Launcher, which
  shows up in your library and starts things like KOReader).
  Do NOT update the Kindle's firmware along the way.

Step 2. Install KOReader
  1. Download koreader-kindlepw2-<version>.zip from
         https://github.com/koreader/koreader/releases
     (or, if your Primer zip's name ends in "with-KOReader", skip this:
     KOReader is already in it).
  2. Connect the Kindle to your computer with a USB cable. It shows up as a
     drive named "Kindle".
  3. Unzip the KOReader zip and copy its "koreader" and "extensions" folders
     onto the Kindle drive.
  4. Eject the Kindle, open KUAL from your library, and tap KOReader to check
     it starts. Exit it again (menu > Exit).

Step 3. Copy Primer onto the Kindle
  1. Connect the Kindle again.
  2. From the unzipped Primer folder, copy "koreader" and "extensions" onto
     the Kindle drive. They merge with the folders already there. If asked,
     replace existing files. (Skip "documents": that's for newer Kindles.)
  3. Eject the Kindle.

Step 4. Start Primer
  Open KUAL and tap KOReader. Primer sets itself up and opens your library,
  and offers to start whenever the Kindle turns on (also in KUAL > Primer,
  and later in Settings > Device options).

Go on to "Adding books" below.


=============================================================================
GUIDE C: REMARKABLE PAPER PRO / PAPER PRO MOVE
=============================================================================

Step 1. Install KOReader
  KOReader runs on the reMarkable through XOVI and AppLoad, which need the
  device in developer mode (turning that on erases the device: back up first).
  Follow the current instructions from the reMarkable community:
      XOVI:     https://github.com/asivery/xovi
      AppLoad:  https://github.com/asivery/rm-appload
  then add KOReader as an AppLoad app. Check that KOReader opens from AppLoad
  before going on.

Step 2. Copy Primer into KOReader's folder
  KOReader's folder on the device is usually
      /home/root/xovi/exthome/appload/koreader
  With the reMarkable plugged in by USB (it's reachable at 10.11.99.1; the
  root password is in Settings > General > Help > About > Copyrights and
  licenses), from the unzipped Primer folder on your computer run:

      scp -r koreader/* root@10.11.99.1:/home/root/xovi/exthome/appload/koreader/

  (Skip "extensions" and "documents": they're Kindle-only.)

Step 3. Start Primer
  KOReader keeps running after you exit it in AppLoad, so restart the device
  (or stop KOReader), then open KOReader from AppLoad. Primer sets itself up
  and opens your library.
  Tip: keep the reMarkable's own orientation lock on. Primer turns pages to
  landscape by itself when you turn the device sideways (Settings > Screen and
  brightness > Turn for landscape).


=============================================================================
GUIDE D: KOBO, POCKETBOOK AND OTHER DEVICES
=============================================================================

  1. Install KOReader the usual way for your device:
         https://github.com/koreader/koreader/wiki/Installation-on-Kobo-devices
     (other devices: the same wiki, "Installation" pages).
  2. Find KOReader's folder on the device. On a Kobo it's ".adds/koreader"
     (a hidden folder: turn on "show hidden files" on your computer).
  3. From the unzipped Primer folder, copy everything INSIDE "koreader"
     (plugins, patches, fonts, screensavers, data) into KOReader's folder,
     merging and replacing. Skip "extensions" and "documents".
  4. Eject, and start KOReader.

  Primer hasn't been tuned on these devices yet. Kindle-only features
  (start at boot, the USB prompt, Kindle sleep screens) don't appear.


=============================================================================
ADDING BOOKS
=============================================================================
  - Use EPUB where you can (MOBI, PDF, FB2, CBZ and TXT open too).
  - Kindle: put books in the "documents" folder. Primer lists every book in
    it, including sub-folders.
  - Kindle AZW3 and KFX files (books bought from Amazon, or sent as AZW3 by
    Calibre) don't open in Primer: KOReader can't render them. Convert them
    to EPUB, for example with Calibre, if they aren't DRM-protected.
  - Primer shows titles and authors from Calibre if you send books with it;
    otherwise from file names like "Title - Author.epub".

DICTIONARY
  Long-press a word to look it up. The release zip includes the English
  Wiktionary (from https://github.com/Vuizur/Wiktionary-Dictionaries,
  CC BY-SA). Any StarDict dictionary works: put its folder in
  koreader/data/dict/.


=============================================================================
UPDATING, UNINSTALLING, TROUBLESHOOTING
=============================================================================

UPDATE
  Copy the folders from the new zip over the old ones, the same way as when
  installing. Your settings, books, highlights and notes are kept.

UNINSTALL
  1. In Primer: Settings > Device options > Start Primer at boot: off.
  2. Connect the device and delete:
       koreader/plugins/voyagepanel.koplugin
       koreader/patches/2-kindle-footer.lua
       koreader/patches/2-kindle-messages.lua
       koreader/patches/2-primer-setup.lua
       koreader/patches/2-primer-ui.lua
       koreader/fonts/primer
       koreader/screensavers/primer
       extensions/primer          (Kindle)
       documents/Primer.sh        (Kindle 5.16.3+)
  KOReader stays installed, with its own interface back.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  - "Primer" doesn't show on the Kindle home screen: wait a minute, then
    restart the Kindle (hold the power button, choose Restart).
  - Primer starts at boot and you want the Kindle's own home screen instead,
    just once: create an empty file named NO_KOREADER in the Kindle's main
    folder (next to "documents"), then restart. Delete it to go back.
  - The Kindle screen seems stuck after exiting Primer: hold the power button
    about 10 seconds to restart, and update Primer (older versions had this
    problem on firmware 5.16.3+).
  - Something else: KOReader writes a log to koreader/crash.log on the device.
    Report problems at https://github.com/skuthus/Primer/issues with that log.
