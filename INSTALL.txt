PRIMER: a modern UI frontend for KOReader
==========================================

Primer gives KOReader a Kindle-style interface: a library home, reading toolbar,
quick settings, Aa panel, dictionary cards, notebook, and settings.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
1. A jailbroken Kindle with KUAL (the Kindle Unified Application Launcher).
   Primer can't do this part: the jailbreak depends on your Kindle's firmware.
   Follow the current community guide at https://kindlemodding.org, and install
   its hotfix so the jailbreak survives Kindle updates.

2. KOReader, unless your Primer zip's name ends in "with-KOReader" (that one
   already includes it). Get it from https://github.com/koreader/koreader/releases:
     - koreader-kindlehf-*.zip  for firmware 5.16.3 or newer
     - koreader-kindlepw2-*.zip for older firmware on a Paperwhite 2 or newer
       (including the Voyage and Oasis)
   Unzip it and copy its folders onto the Kindle, the same way as step 2 below.

   Primer was made for KOReader 2026.07 or newer.


INSTALL
-------
1. Connect the Kindle to your computer with a USB cable.
2. Copy the "koreader" and "extensions" folders from this zip onto the Kindle.
   They merge with the folders already there; if asked, replace existing files.
3. Eject the Kindle.
4. Open KUAL and tap KOReader.

Primer sets itself up on its first start and opens on your library. A welcome
screen offers to start Primer whenever the Kindle turns on (you can change this
later in Settings > Device options, or in KUAL > Primer).

Dictionary: Primer uses any StarDict dictionary in koreader/data/dict/ on the
Kindle. If your zip didn't include one, the free English Wiktionary from
https://github.com/Vuizur/Wiktionary-Dictionaries works well: unpack it and copy
its folder there.


UPDATE
------
Copy the folders from the new zip over the old ones, as when installing. Your
settings, books and notes are kept.


UNINSTALL
---------
1. In Primer: Settings > Device options > Start Primer at boot: off.
2. Connect the Kindle and delete:
     koreader/plugins/voyagepanel.koplugin
     koreader/patches/2-kindle-footer.lua
     koreader/patches/2-kindle-messages.lua
     koreader/patches/2-primer-setup.lua
     koreader/patches/2-primer-ui.lua
     koreader/fonts/primer
     koreader/screensavers/primer
     extensions/primer
KOReader stays installed, with its own interface back.


ON OTHER DEVICES
----------------
Primer runs on other KOReader devices too (Kobo, PocketBook…): copy the
"koreader" folder's contents into KOReader's folder on the device and skip the
"extensions" folder (it's Kindle-only). Layouts are tuned for the Kindle Voyage
and may need adjusting elsewhere.
