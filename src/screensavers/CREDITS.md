# Sleep screen collections

All images are public domain (the artists died more than 100 years ago and the works were published before 1930).
Sources are Wikimedia Commons scans, scaled to fill 1072×1448 (centre-cropped, no white bars) and converted to greyscale for the Kindle Voyage.
